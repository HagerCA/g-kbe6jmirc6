How to Read This

Synastry is not compatibility scoring. A tight square is not a warning and a tight trine is not a blessing. The orb tells you how loud a contact is, not whether it is good. Chris and Pixie have the most contacts inside one degree of any pair here, including an exact Mars-Pluto square, and they have been together since 2006.

Count the personal planets separately from the outer ones. Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus and Mars contacts create the feeling of instant recognition. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto contacts work by weather: slower, structural, felt over years rather than minutes. Two people can have forty contacts and almost no personal-planet contact, which produces a real bond that simply does not arrive pre-assembled. That is the Chris and Hendrix situation, and it is the single most actionable thing on this page.

The angles matter more than most people expect. Ascendant and Midheaven contacts are about the actual spot of ground someone was born on at a specific minute. They are the most time-sensitive points in any chart, which means they are also the first thing to go wrong when a birth time is off by an hour. Three weeks of this family's chart data said Hendrix had a Scorpio Ascendant. It did not.

Every number on this page is computed from the four birth records. The prose is interpretation and is labelled as such by being prose. Where a sentence makes a claim about geometry, the orb is printed next to it so you can check it.
