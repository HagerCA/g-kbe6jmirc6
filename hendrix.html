The Wheel

Click any planet to isolate its aspects. Click a house number in the tables to jump to that house. The outer band is the zodiac, coloured by element. The inner spokes are your Placidus house cusps, which are unequal because houses are a projection of a tilted, spinning Earth, not twelve equal slices.

How to read this

The left-hand point is your Ascendant, Libra 20°14'. That is the degree of the zodiac that was climbing over the eastern horizon at the minute you were born. Everything counts counterclockwise from there.

The bottom is the IC and the top is the Midheaven, Cancer 22°18'. Planets near the top were high in the sky at your birth. Planets near the bottom were underneath the Earth.

The lines across the middle are aspects, the geometric relationships between planets. Red is friction, blue is flow, gold is fusion, grey is minor. Thicker means tighter.

The Four Angles

The angles are not planets. They are the orientation of the sky to the exact spot of ground you were born on, which is why your birth time and city matter so much. Change the minute and these move.
