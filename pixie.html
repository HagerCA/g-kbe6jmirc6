The four angles

Where the sky meets the ground

The angles are not planets. They are the orientation of the sky to the exact spot of ground at the exact minute of birth: the horizon (Ascendant and Descendant) and the meridian (Midheaven and IC). Change the minute and they move, which is why they are the most personal points in a chart and the first to go wrong with a bad birth time. Each card shows what the angle is, how your sign colors it, what in the chart touches it, and which of the family land on it.

Reading the numbers

Angle, orb, strength

The angle is how far apart two planets sit on the zodiac, 0° to 180°. The orb is how far that is from an exact aspect. The strength is the orb as a share of the allowance: inside one chart, 8° for a conjunction or opposition, 7° for a square or trine, 5° for a sextile, and less for the minor aspects. The buttons are your three tightest major aspects.
